# Syllabus Checklist

All PSU syllabi should minimally contain the following components:

- [ ] Course number and title, # of credits, semester and year
- [ ] Instructor name, contact information, office hours
- [ ] Course Description: the formally accepted course description as printed in the catalog
- [ ] Pre-requisites and/or co-requisites
- [ ] Additional descriptors
- [ ] General Education (if applicable): the catalog description of applicable General Education attribute(s)
- [ ] Student Learning Outcomes: same for all sections of a course
- [ ] Required readings and materials
- [ ] Course Outline
- [ ] Assignments and Activities (including the date of the final exam or final activity)
- [ ] OSHA Standards/Safety regulations (if applicable)
- [ ] Attendance Policy: a short statement. Include the link to the Excused Absence Policy
  - General link to the policy page: <https://coursecatalog.plymouth.edu/university-policies-procedures/>
  - Link to PDF of absence policy: <https://www.plymouth.edu/sites/default/files/media/2022-11/psu-excused-absence-policy.pdf>
- [ ] Grading criteria/standards: a short statement. Include the link to the Fair Grading Policy
  - General link to the policy page: <https://coursecatalog.plymouth.edu/university-policies-procedures/>
  - Link to PDF of Fair Grading Policy: <https://www.plymouth.edu/sites/default/files/media/2022-11/psu-fair-grading-policy.pdf>
- [ ] Academic honesty: a short statement. Include the link to the Academic Integrity Policy
  - General link to the policy page: <https://coursecatalog.plymouth.edu/university-policies-procedures/>
  - Link to PDF of Academic Integrity Policy: <https://www.plymouth.edu/sites/default/files/media/2022-11/psu-academic-integrity-policy.pdf>
- [ ] ADA Statement: Plymouth State University is committed to providing students with documented disabilities equal access to all university programs and facilities. If you think you have a disability requiring accommodations, you should contact Campus Accessibility Services (CAS), located in Speare 210 (535-3300) to determine whether you are eligible for such accommodations. Academic accommodations will only be considered for students who have registered with CAS. If you have a Letter of Accommodation for this course from CAS, please provide the instructor with that information privately so that you and the instructor can review those accommodations.
- [ ] Other: for instance, many like to add statements about what to do if there is inclement weather/closings, or personal policies about classroom etiquette, etc.
