## Boolean Logic and Algebra

- Computers store data as bits.
- A bit distinguishes two values, which we can talk about as 0/1, low/high,
true/false, red/blue, etc.

Especially interesting interpretation: true/false

- Reasoning about true/false isn't new. Philosophers have been trying to figure
that out for thousands of years.
- George Boole formalized true/false reasoning mathematically in his 1847 book.
That's before computers and even before much practical application of
electricity (although the telegraph was deployed, electric motors existed, and
there were bad electric light bulbs).

Boolean Logic Basics

- Three operations: AND, OR, NOT
- Truth tables
- Different ways to write expressions (at least three notation)
