How to study with an LLM? Very intentionally.

First, let's distinguish two different scenarios:

- Preparing for a constrained task (e.g. exam, essay, homework, presentation)
- Preparing for an open-ended task (e.g. a larger project, including class
research paper type tasks)

These are similar, but there's one big difference: for the second category,
you'll need to discover sources, techniques, etc that you didn't get any hints
on beforehand.

Both use a similar workflow. You need to work through your problems and your
sources until you've got solutions to your problems, possibly with direct
attribution to your sources. The more complex workflow just includes adding both
problems and sources as you go.

Concrete example 1: Studying for a Calc Quiz

The quiz will be on:

- https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/3-5-derivatives-of-trigonometric-functions
