## Planning

- We started by discussing Agile methods, which are a way to avoid over-planning.
- But under-planning is also a problem.

So how do we plan without planning?

**Goals**

- Identify goals.
- Once we have goals, we can identify intermediate goals that we'll clearly need
to accomplish to get there.

**Deadlines**

- Agile strategy looks like it completely avoids deadlines. You just see what
you can get done and try to maximize velocity.
- But sometimes there are deadlines.

So:

- So goals have associated deadlines.
- You try to figure out if you're on track.
- When it's clear you can't accomplish the goal on time, or like you'll have
extra time to hit stretch goals, you adjust the goals to match the time budget.
