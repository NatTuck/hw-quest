**First Game**

We're going to get together in teams of two and start working on our first game.

What game? A simple path following game.

- https://boardgamegeek.com/image/633254/candy-land
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snakes_and_ladders

You need:

- A piece of paper
- A pencil
- A path drawn on the piece of paper
- Spaces along the path
- Players start their tokens at the beginning of the path
- When they get to the end they win
- How do they move their tokens along the path?
- How is this game not super boring?

We'll be working on this for the rest of the period *and* as homework due next
week. Make the best path following game you can, and explain what choices you
made and why.

Your goal for *today* is a cellphone picture of a piece of paper uploaded to
Github with some notes.

Your goal for the homework due next week is whatever you think makes this most
interesting. That could be well thought out rules really fancy game board
art, both, or something else.
