## In Class Questions

### Categorizing Games

1. **Chess** — Abstract strategy, turn-based, 2-player, perfect information, zero-sum, traditional board game.
2. **Quake** — First-person shooter, twitch reflexes/spatial awareness, real-time, digital multiplayer.
3. **Bocce** — Physical lawn game / precision sport, spatial estimation, turn-based, low-cardio dexterity.
4. **Super Meat Boy** — Digital precision platformer, single-player, rapid trial-and-error, muscle memory.
5. **Pokémon GO** — Augmented reality (AR), location-based mobile, collection/RPG, blended physical/digital.
6. **Magic: The Gathering** (or **Pokémon TCG**) — Collectible card game (CCG), hidden information, metagame deck construction, asynchronous design vs. synchronous play.
7. **Tag** — Folk game, zero equipment, continuous real-time, high-cardio physical agility, playground rule negotiation.
8. **Dungeons & Dragons** — Tabletop role-playing game (TTRPG), cooperative storytelling, asymmetric authority (Dungeon Master vs. players), open-ended win conditions.
9. **Among Us** — Digital social deduction, asymmetric roles, imperfect information, heavy emphasis on out-of-game conversation.
10. **Tetris** — Digital abstract falling-block puzzle, spatial logic under time pressure, survival/high-score (no traditional "win" state).
11. **Minecraft** (Creative / Survival) — Digital sandbox, emergent gameplay; frequently straddles the line between "game" and "toy/canvas."
12. **Texas Hold’em Poker** — Standard 52-card deck, incomplete information, wagering/resource management, psychological bluffing.
13. **Twister** — Physical dexterity/flexibility game, player bodies as the game pieces, spatial entanglement.
14. **The Stanley Parable** — "Walking simulator" / interactive narrative, meta-commentary, virtually no mechanical challenge or traditional failure state.
15. **Jenga** — Physical balance and fine motor dexterity, literal physics-based mechanics, sudden-death elimination.
16. **Escape Room** (Live-Action) — Physical environment as interface, cooperative puzzle-solving, strict time limit, non-replayable.
17. **Cookie Clicker** — Incremental / idle game, minimal mechanical input, math optimization; tests the boundary of player agency.
18. **Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes** — Asymmetric cooperative puzzle, cross-medium (one player on digital screen, other reading a physical paper manual), pure communication bottleneck.
19. **Roulette** — Pure chance/gambling, zero strategic agency after the wager, tests the line between "game" and "gambling mechanic."
20. **Soccer (Association Football)** — Institutionalized team sport, strict physical rules, official governing body, high physical conditioning.

* Each game: Digital or Physical
* Which games are sports?
* Is **Cookie Clicker** a game if it plays itself?
* Is **The Stanley Parable** a game if you cannot "lose"?
* Is **Minecraft (Creative Mode)** a game or a digital Lego set?
* **Alternative Taxonomies:** Ask them to ignore platform/genre and categorize by:
* **Core skill required** (Dexterity, Logic/Calculation, Social/Deception, Spatial, Chance).
* **Player alignment** (Competitive 1v1, Team vs. Team, Pure Co-op, 1-vs-Many asymmetric, Solo).
* **Information state** (Perfect/Open information vs. Imperfect/Hidden information).
